# Writing the ad script

A short ad has one job: make the right person stop scrolling, want the thing, and know exactly how to get it. Every scene earns its place or gets cut.

## Pick a structure

**A. Problem → Solution (default, best for services and everyday products)**
hook → problem → product → benefits → (stat/testimonial if real) → price → cta

**B. Offer first (best for sales, promos, launches)**
hook (states the deal) → product → price → benefits → cta

**C. Proof first (best when the user has strong reviews or numbers)**
hook → stat or testimonial → product → benefits → price → cta

**D. Quick 15-second (WhatsApp Status, retargeting)**
hook → product → price → cta

## Hooks (scene 1, the most important line)

Max 7 words. Speak to the viewer about their situation, not about the brand. Patterns that work:

- Question about a pain: "Too tired to *cook* tonight?"
- Call out the audience: "Lagos *small business* owners, look"
- Bold claim: "Your skin will *thank* you"
- Price shock: "Full meal for *₦4,500*?"
- Stop/warning: "Stop paying *full price* for data"
- Curiosity: "The *jollof* everyone's talking about"

Write 3 hooks, pick the strongest, keep the other two for A/B variants.

## Text limits per scene (so it reads in time)

| Scene | Limit |
|---|---|
| hook | ≤ 7 words, optional `sub` ≤ 5 words |
| problem | title ≤ 3 words; 2–4 lines, ≤ 5 words each |
| product | title ≤ 6 words; subtitle ≤ 6 words; badge ≤ 2 words |
| benefits | 2–4 items, ≤ 6 words each; lead with the outcome ("At your door in 45 mins"), not the feature |
| stat | value like "5,000+", "4.9★", "10 yrs"; label ≤ 4 words |
| price | price exactly as given; note ≤ 5 words |
| testimonial | quote ≤ 18 words, real name/initial, real place or role |
| cta | ≤ 5 words; button ≤ 4 words; 1–3 contacts; urgency ≤ 4 words and only if true |

People can't pause a reel to read. If a line needs more words, split it into two scenes or cut it.

## Highlights

Highlight 1–2 words per line with `*asterisks*`: the word carrying the emotion or the deal (cook, delivered hot, ₦4,500, free). Highlighting everything highlights nothing.

## Honesty rules (non-negotiable)

- Testimonials, ratings, customer counts and "as seen on" claims must come from the user. Never invent them. No real proof → use structure A, B or D without those scenes.
- Urgency ("ends Sunday", "only 20 left") only when the user confirms it is true.
- No before/after health or weight-loss claims, no "cures", no guaranteed income claims. These also get ads rejected by Meta and TikTok.
- Prices, phone numbers, handles: copy exactly.

## Local market tips (Nigeria / West Africa)

- Put the price in ₦ with a comma: ₦4,500. Prices are a strong hook here; don't hide them.
- "Chat us on WhatsApp" outperforms "visit our website" for most small businesses. Use the `chat` icon with the number.
- Delivery area and speed are major buying reasons. Name the area (Lekki, Ikeja, nationwide).
- Light Pidgin can work in hooks for street-level brands ("Hunger no go catch you today"), but keep prices and contacts in plain English. Match the brand's own voice.
- Many viewers watch with sound off: the on-screen text must tell the full story on its own.

## Brand colors when the user has none

Pick from the product's world and keep strong contrast:
- Food: dark `#14100D` background, orange `#FF7A1A` primary, yellow `#FFD23F` accent
- Beauty/natural: cream `#FBF6F0`, green `#2E7D5B`, gold `#E9C46A`
- Fashion/luxury: black `#0E0E10`, gold `#C9A227`, ivory accent `#F2E8D5`
- Tech/fintech: navy `#0B1630`, electric blue `#3B82F6`, lime `#B6F23C`
- Kids/fun: white `#FFFFFF`, magenta `#E5007E`, sky `#29B6F6`, dark text `#1A1A1A`
